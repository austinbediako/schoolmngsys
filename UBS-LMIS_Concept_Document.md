# UBS-LMIS Concept Document

**University Basic School Legon Integrated Management Information System**

## Project Overview

UBS-LMIS is a centralized management platform for University Basic School, Legon, supporting the Nursery, Kindergarten, Primary, and JHS departments.

## Background

The system digitizes student, academic, financial, and administrative processes across the school, from Nursery through JHS 3.

## Regulatory Context

The school's academic calendar, curriculum, and assessment structure follow national frameworks: the **Ghana Education Service (GES)** governs the school calendar and general policy, the **National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA)** sets the subject curriculum, and the **West African Examinations Council (WAEC)** administers the BECE at the end of JHS 3. The system should be built to stay aligned with these frameworks rather than a generic academic model.

## Problem Statement

Manual processes make record keeping, communication, reporting, attendance, examinations, and fee management inefficient.

## Goal

Develop a secure, integrated School Management Information System tailored to the operational needs of University Basic School, Legon.

## Objectives

- Digitize student records
- Automate attendance and results
- Improve parent communication
- Manage fees
- Provide analytics
- Reduce paperwork

## Users

School Administration, Heads of Department, Teachers, Students, Parents/Guardians, Accountant, Librarian, School Nurse.

## Academic Structure

- Nursery 1
- Nursery 2
- KG 1
- KG 2
- Primary 1 to Primary 6 *(also referred to as Basic 1–6)*
- JHS 1 to JHS 3 *(also referred to as Basic 7–9)*

Together, KG 1 through JHS 3 make up Ghana's 11-year compulsory Basic Education cycle under FCUBE. The school runs a **three-term academic year**; note that JHS 3's Term 2 and Term 3 typically end earlier than the rest of the school to allow time for BECE revision — the Timetable module should support this as a separate calendar variant rather than a single school-wide calendar.

## Expected Benefits

Improved efficiency, centralized records, better communication, faster reporting, stronger academic monitoring, and data-driven decision making.

## MVP

Authentication, Student Management, Teacher Management, Class Management, Attendance, Results, Fees, Parent Portal.
